Product description

Möller’s cod liver oil with natural flavour is a good source of omega-3 fatty acids DHA and EPA as well as vitamins A, D and E. Möller’s cod liver oil is made from wild caught Northeast Arctic cod, that is particularly rich in omega-3 fatty acids.

These nutrients are important for the normal development of brain, heart, vision and help maintain a normal immune system.

Möller’s omega-3 cod liver oil is recognised by the International Taste Institute in Brussels, Belgium and has been awarded the Superior Taste Award.

The recommended daily dose for adults and children from age of three years is 5ml (teaspoon).

Ingredients: Cod liver oil, dl-α-tocopheryl acetate (vitamin E), antioxidant (tocopherol-rich extract).

DHA omega-3 fatty acid contributes to maintenance of normal vision* and normal brain function*. DHA and EPA contribute to the normal function of the heart**. Vitamin D contributes to normal absorption of calcium and phosphorus and is needed for normal growth and development of bone in children. Vitamin D contributes to the normal function of the immune system.

*The beneficial effect is achieved with daily consumption of 250 mg of DHA.

**The beneficial effect is achieved with daily consumption of 250 mg of EPA and DHA.

About us

Orkla Health AS  is the Nordic region’s leading supplier (and marketeer) of health products ranging from omega-3 and vitamin & mineral supplements. Our mission is healthy living made easier and we aim to meet specific health needs at every stage of the consumer’s life. The company operates in the Nordics, Baltics and Poland, but is also represented in several other countries across the world with different brands and products. Orkla Health is part of the Orkla Care holding.
